[04] // motion

Motion rules

  • Reveal

    Fade up 28px from an 8px blur over 900ms, staggered 90ms between siblings. The reveal class is removed afterwards, so hover stays instant.

  • Hover

    Lift 4px plus a soft cyan glow; 3D tilt of at most 5–7° on cards. Mouse only; never on touch.

  • Data

    Charts grow from their baseline once, when they scroll into view; numbers count up over 1.4s with an ease-out curve.

  • Ambient

    Background effects (drifting glows, network pulses, marquee) run slowly and pause when off-screen or in a background tab.
